How to Convert PDF to Word Online

Convert PDF files into editable Word documents with a practical workflow. Learn when PDF-to-Word works best and what to do with scanned PDFs.

When should you convert a PDF to Word?

PDF is designed to preserve layout, while Word is easier to edit. Converting is useful when you need to revise paragraphs, reuse text, or continue editing a document.

Simple workflow

  1. Open the PDF to Word tool.
  2. Select your PDF file.
  3. Run the conversion and download the generated document.
  4. Open the result in Word or another compatible editor and check headings, tables, spacing, and page breaks.
Scanned PDF?

If the PDF contains scanned pages rather than selectable text, OCR may be needed. Try OCR or Image to Text for text extraction.

Tips for better results

  • Use the original, highest-quality PDF when possible.
  • Check complex tables and unusual fonts after conversion.
  • For very large or damaged PDFs, try extracting or compressing the file first.
