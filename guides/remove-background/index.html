How to Remove an Image Background

Understand when automatic background removal is useful and how to prepare an image for cleaner results.

Best images for background removal

Images with a clear subject and reasonable contrast between the subject and background are generally easier for an automated remover to process.

Preparation checklist

  • Use the clearest original image available.
  • Make sure the main subject is visible.
  • Avoid extremely tiny subjects or heavy blur.
  • Review edges around hair, transparent objects, and similar fine details.

After removing the background

You may want to crop the result, resize it for a specific destination, or compress it before uploading.
