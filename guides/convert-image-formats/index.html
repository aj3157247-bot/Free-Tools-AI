How to Convert JPG, PNG, and WebP Images

Understand common image formats and choose the right converter for websites, editing, transparency, and compatibility.

JPG, PNG, or WebP?

JPG is widely supported and works well for photographs. PNG supports transparency and is useful for graphics that need crisp edges. WebP is a modern web-friendly format that can offer smaller files for many images.

Common conversion choices

JPG → PNG

Useful when you need PNG output or transparency workflows.

PNG → JPG

Useful when a JPG is required or transparency is not needed.

JPG → WebP

Useful for web delivery where WebP is supported.

WebP → JPG

Useful when broader compatibility is needed.

Before converting

Keep an original copy when quality matters. Converting between lossy formats repeatedly can reduce image quality.
